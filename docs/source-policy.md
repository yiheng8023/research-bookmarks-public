# Source Policy

This repository prefers sources that are:

- official project or product websites;
- official documentation;
- canonical source repositories;
- standards bodies;
- public institutional references;
- clearly licensed public knowledge resources.

Avoid treating popularity as proof of quality. A reviewed secondary or
community reference may remain public-safe, but it must not be labeled as an
official or canonical source. Legal ownership may remain `needs_review`; the
catalogue must not infer a legal entity from a hostname.

Workspace/common-entry folders and domestic/international folders are views,
not resource owners. Public records use one primary subject category plus
`entry_role` and `market_scope`; region labels do not belong in canonical
titles.

External discovery consumers may use this repository as a seed, but they must
still perform their own lifecycle, deduplication, license, and quality checks.
