# Sponsoring

Sponsorship is voluntary. Any amount is appreciated and supports ongoing
maintenance, documentation, testing, and community operations.

Reviewed sponsorship channels:

- CNY: the WeChat Pay and Alipay QR codes in [README.md](README.md).
- Cross-border or other supported currencies:
  [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/ncp/payment/LNTF8KXGJXMZY). Available
  currencies, payment methods, conversion, and fees are determined by PayPal
  and may vary by country or region.

Verify the displayed recipient before confirming a payment.

Do not send funds through accounts or links that are not published in this
repository. Sponsorship is not a charitable donation or tax deduction and does
not purchase a support SLA, security warranty, response priority, source
admission, release timing, features, or control over technical decisions.
